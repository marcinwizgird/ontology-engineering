Development plan · 4 weeks · 2 to 27 November 2026

Naive GraphRAG Delivery Plan

In four weeks ASE designs, generates, indexes and evaluates a working naive GraphRAG agent over a real document corpus in FalkorDB. Both the indexer and the query agent are generated from library patterns, not hand-written.

Assumptions: weeks start Monday 2 November 2026; owners are the architecture owner (A) and the delivery owner (D), with points sized for two to three engineers. Ticket ids (GR-n) are local; JIRA assigns its own keys on import.

Goal and definition of done

"Naive" means the simplest GraphRAG that is still honest: entities and relations are extracted from chunks at indexing time; at query time, question terms are linked to graph entities, their neighbourhood is expanded a bounded number of hops, and the chunks attached to those entities are passed to the synthesizer with citations. No community summaries, no global search, no graph learning.

The 4-week goal is met when all of these hold:

  1. cli run --project RS-11 --online selects NaiveGraphRag, passes the gate and the sweep, generates the indexer and the agent, builds the kg:RS-11 graph in FalkorDB, and answers the RS-11 eval set with citations.
  2. On the RS-11 eval set: cross-document recall and citation recall beat the word-overlap baseline on the same questions; unanswerable questions are refused.
  3. The same run works offline (no key, no FalkorDB) with a deterministic extractor and an in-memory graph, so CI stays green.
  4. cli selftest is OK; regenerating the programs gives byte-identical files; models/ changed only through the reviewed library commit in week 1.
  5. Every design choice below is a Decision node with owner, date, reason and requirement.

Target design

Two new library patterns. GraphIndexing runs once per corpus (batch workload); NaiveGraphRag runs per question (interactive). Keeping them separate lets the analysis budget each on its own terms.

ElementPropertiesPurpose

Indexes: full-text on Entity.name and Entity.aliases; range on Chunk.id and Entity.key. All Cypher is parameterised. The kg:* graphs are product data, kept apart from ASE's own ase and ase-designs graphs.

Week 1 · 2 to 6 November

Make every later step possible: provider-agnostic model calls, generated programs that can carry new state and parallel branches, and the library, spec and selection for GraphRAG.

Week 2 · 9 to 13 November

Turn a corpus into a knowledge graph in FalkorDB, through a generated indexing program, with an offline twin for tests.

Week 3 · 16 to 20 November

Answer questions from the graph: link, expand, collect, cite. Start measuring.

Week 4 · 23 to 27 November

Prove it end to end, make it inspectable, and close with the review gate and a demo.
